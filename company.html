Our Mission

Construction is one of the least digitized industries in the world. We're here to change that — for the firms that get left behind.

The enterprise GCs have McKinsey, Deloitte, and armies of internal innovation teams. Small and mid-size contractors — the firms doing $10M to $150M a year, the ones actually building most of America's buildings — have almost nothing. No dedicated tech staff. No AI strategy. No one showing them how to compete.

Plumb exists to close that gap. We bring field-tested construction expertise and production-grade AI capability to the firms that need it most. We work alongside your team, tie our fees to outcomes, and don't leave until the work is done.
